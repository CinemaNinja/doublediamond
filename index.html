Interior designer support

Receiving, inspection, storage, and white-glove installation for design projects.

Double Diamond helps designers keep projects moving from first shipment through final placement, with warehouse receiving, photo documentation, temperature-controlled storage, inventory tracking, and careful installations across Colorado mountain communities.

  • Warehouse receiving with full item inspections and condition notes.
  • Digital photos and online inventory support for project visibility.
  • Temperature-controlled storage for furniture, art, rugs, and accessories.
  • Release coordination, delivery sequencing, and final installation.
  • Art hanging, hoists, debris removal, and finish-level site care.

Designer workflow

Online inventory, warehouse receiving, and custom installation support.

These expanded services carry over the detail from Double Diamond's original Interior Designer Services page, keeping project communication, inspection, storage, release, and installation work in one organized path.

01
Online Inventory System

Web-accessible proprietary software keeps Double Diamond and the interior designer aligned from receiving through storage, release, installation, and communication.

  • Login access to the receiving report for each project.
  • Photos, item details, condition notes, locations, and release status in one place.
  • Email notifications when the report is updated or damage is documented.
02
Warehouse Receiving

Every shipment is opened, unpacked, inspected, photographed, and cataloged so designers have documentation before an item moves into storage or installation planning.

  • Visible freight damage is marked on the bill of lading and photographed.
  • Each received item is matched to project notes, location, condition, and storage needs.
  • Advance account setup helps the warehouse receive designer freight cleanly.
03
Full Custom Installations

Install crews release items from storage, confirm inventory numbers, sequence the load, and place furniture, rugs, art, and accessories with finish-level care.

  • Rugs can be loaded last and placed first to support the full install sequence.
  • Furniture setup, accessory placement, art hanging, hoists, and crane coordination are supported when needed.
  • Debris removal and careful protection help preserve the interior integrity of new or remodeled homes.

Residential and commercial moves

Professional crews for careful packing, moving, assembly, and specialty pieces.

From homes and condos to commercial spaces, hotels, schools, restaurants, and events, Double Diamond sends prepared crews with the equipment and judgment needed for complex mountain-town access.

  • Household and commercial moving, packing, and unpacking.
  • Furniture disassembly, reassembly, stretch wrap, and protection.
  • Foreman-led crews with Double Diamond employees on site.
  • Walk ramps, straps, liftgates, tools, and clean furniture blankets.
  • Licensed and insured operations for valuable moves and specialty pieces.

Contractor logistics

Receiving and delivery support for windows, cabinets, flooring, and project materials.

Contractors can route high-value or bulky materials to Double Diamond for organized receiving, warehousing, and staged delivery so crews are not stuck waiting on freight or crowded by early arrivals.

  • Receiving for windows, cabinets, flooring, fixtures, and finish materials.
  • Short-term warehousing until the site is ready for installation.
  • Scheduled delivery windows that support active construction timelines.
  • Heavy and oversized material handling with warehouse equipment.
  • Cleaner jobsites and fewer contractor deadline collisions.

Art handling

Careful handling for single works, full collections, exhibits, crating, and installation.

The team supports collectors, designers, galleries, and homeowners with the calm, documented handling that art and fragile objects need from pickup through placement.

  • Single pieces, complete collections, exhibits, and gallery-scale projects.
  • Warehouse receiving and secure temperature-controlled storage.
  • Custom crating, packing, shipping coordination, and transport.
  • Installation support for homes, galleries, and designer projects.
  • Condition-aware handling for fragile, oversized, or high-value works.
